# Translation Notes (Khmer & English)

This document records the terminology choices, phonetic nuances, and recommended review points for native Khmer speakers and Cambodian venue managers.

## Key Inventory & Business Terms

| English Term | Khmer Term | Pronunciation Guide | Context & Rationales |
|---|---|---|---|
| Stock / Inventory | ស្តុក / បញ្ជីស្តុកទំនិញ | *Stok / Banh-ji Stok Tum-ninh* | Commonly used in Cambodian businesses. "ស្តុក" is the standard loanword for physical stock. |
| Customer Leftovers | ទំនិញផ្ញើរបស់ភ្ញៀវ | *Tum-ninh Phnheu Roboh Pnhiev* | Refers to drinks, beer cartons, or bottles left behind by karaoke guests for subsequent visits. |
| Held for Customer | កំពុងផ្ញើទុក | *Kompung Phnheu Tuk* | Status of leftover items stored safely in the venue's lockers/shelves. |
| Partially Collected | បានមកយកខ្លះ | *Ban Mok Yok Khlah* | When a guest collects a portion of their held items (e.g., 6 of 12 beers). |
| Fully Collected | បានមកយកអស់ | *Ban Mok Yok Oh* | When all remaining held items have been retrieved. |
| Physical Stock | ស្តុកជាក់ស្តែង | *Stok Cheak Sdaeng* | Total on-hand count present physically in the venue. |
| Reserved Stock | ស្តុកបម្រុង | *Stok Bom-rung* | Quantity reserved for customer collection; excluded from saleable inventory. |
| Available Stock | ស្តុកអាចលក់បាន | *Stok Arch Lork Ban* | Net stock ready for room orders (`Physical Stock - Reserved Stock`). |
| Stock In | នាំចូលស្តុក | *Noam Chol Stok* | Goods receipt from distributors or cash purchases. |
| Stock Out | នាំចេញស្តុក | *Noam Chenh Stok* | Dispensing beverages or ingredients to karaoke rooms or venue operations. |
| Returned Items | ទំនិញបង្វិលចូល | *Tum-ninh Bong-vel Chol* | Sealed and undamaged goods returned from rooms back to inventory. |
| Supplier | អ្នកផ្គត់ផ្គង់ | *Neak Pkhot Pkhong* | Beverage and raw material vendors. |
| Weighted Average Cost | ថ្លៃដើមមធ្យមភាគ | *Tlay Derm Mot-chhom-pheak* | Inventory accounting valuation method. |

## Khmer Numeral & Phone Input Rules

1. **Numerals**:
   - Western digits (`0, 1, 2...`) are the default in Cambodian commercial accounting.
   - The system supports Khmer numerals (`០, ១, ២, ៣, ៤, ៥, ៦, ៧, ៨, ៩`) for localized display and search inputs.
2. **Phone Number Normalization**:
   - Cambodian phone numbers are entered with leading zero (`012 345 678`), with country code (`+855 12 345 678`), or using Khmer digits (`០១២៣៤៥៦៧៨`).
   - Normalization converts Khmer digits to Western digits and standardizes to local (`0xxxxxxxx`) and international (`+855xxxxxxxx`) forms for index matching.

## Review Notes for Native Speaker

1. **Storage Location**: Currently rendered as `ទីតាំងទុក / ទូ / ធ្នើ` (Storage Location / Locker / Shelf). If the venue uses specific numbered lockers (e.g. "ទូផ្ញើលេខ..."), this label can be tailored.
2. **Room Order / Dispense Reason**: Rendered as `បម្រើភ្ញៀវក្នុងបន្ទប់ / ការលក់`.
3. **Audit Log Actions**: Rendered in viewer's active language from stored neutral enums.
